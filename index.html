Abstract & Core Defense Rationale

This defense portal represents the empirical milestones achieved during the scientific practice in Kazakh Speech Technologies. The research explores two critical vectors:

  • Colloquial Robustness: Benchmarking ASR models on Shala-Kazakh code-switching dialects, revealing a 15-20% average drop in WER compared to pure literary speech.
  • Computational Optimization: Transitioning speech synthesis from autoregressive giants to high-efficiency ONNX runtime architectures, achieving low-latency deployment.

Key Scientific Outputs

Developed a robust filler-cleaning pipeline for acoustic speech corpora. The resulting cleaned lexicons are ready to be packaged directly into custom TTS datasets (e.g. VibeVoice).

Task 4: Flow Matching Pipeline Architecture

Flow matching (F5-TTS, Tote-TTS) represents the state-of-the-art alternative to autoregressive architectures. It uses ODE solvers to continuously match the speech probability density flow.

Text Input & Phonemizer
Normalizes Kazakh orthography and punctuation into discrete IPA phoneme representations.
Continuous Flow Matcher
Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE) framework matching probability distribution trajectories.
ONNX / HiFi-GAN Vocoder
